# [[bold the words a skimmer should catch]]

Bold picks out the important parts so someone skimming a note still gets the gist. Part of [[owner writing and note preferences]]

- Bold the few words that carry the point, like the key claim or the one fact not to miss
- Reading only the headings and the bold should give a fair idea of the note, which goes hand in hand with [[headings and short bullets make notes scannable]]
- For a step more emphasis than bold, use an ==highlight== on the part that really can't be missed
- Labels belong in headings, not in bold mini-headings at the start of a bullet
