# [[every question carries its own context]]

- A question to {owner} names the thing it's asking about, so they can answer it without scrolling back to work out what "that" means
- Part of [[owner collaboration preferences]]

## why

- A bare "that", "this", or "it" makes {owner} re-read earlier text to work out the subject before they can answer
- A question that names its subject can stand on its own, the same way [[claim titles make a note reusable wherever it's linked]]

## how

- Put the subject in the question itself: "Should I add a plain line above the embed in all 15 agent notes?" instead of "Should I do that instead?"
- When there are options, name each one in a few plain words, as in [[open questions get a spot for answers]]
- This holds in chat, in notes, and in Tincan callouts
