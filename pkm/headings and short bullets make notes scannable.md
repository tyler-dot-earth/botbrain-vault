# [[headings and short bullets make notes scannable]]

The owner skims notes, so they're shaped to be scanned: headings say what each section is about, and short bullets carry the explanation. Part of [[owner writing and note preferences]]

## the shape

- Use descriptive `##` and `###` headings to label sections and ideas
- Put the explanation in bullets under a heading instead of bold inline labels as mini-headings
- Keep each bullet to one sentence and leave off its terminal period
- Keep lists to a handful of bullets, splitting a long one under another heading

## not everything is an outline

- A short lead sentence helps orient the reader
- Prose, quotes, embeds, and visuals all fit when they add something, since we [[pick the simplest visual that makes it clearer]]
