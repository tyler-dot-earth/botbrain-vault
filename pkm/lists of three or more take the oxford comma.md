# [[lists of three or more take the oxford comma]]

This vault uses the Oxford comma, so a list of three or more items gets a comma before the last "and" or "or". Part of [[owner writing and note preferences]]

- Write "notes, links, and bases", not "notes, links and bases"
- It keeps the last two items from reading as one pair
