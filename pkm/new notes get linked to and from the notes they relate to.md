# [[new notes get linked to and from the notes they relate to]]

- Consider links when adding a note, including links out to related notes and links back from them
- No link is required: add connections that fit and leave a note unlinked when none do
- [Andy Matuschak on densely linked evergreen notes](https://notes.andymatuschak.org/zF8xCU4BwXwbmSyp7tmff9i) describes how looking for connections prompts fresh thinking and revisiting older notes
- Andy also finds it freeing to link to a stub before the note exists
- Part of [[owner writing and note preferences]], and it's how [[structure grows from links not folders]]

## in practice

- When a new note has a useful connection, link it from related notes or an area hub, and link out where that helps
- When updating a note gives it a new relation, often do the same for that relation
- Each link goes inline in a sentence, since [[a link sits beside the point it supports]] and [[claim titles make a note reusable wherever it's linked]]
- Link only where it genuinely fits, and skip forced links
- Point at a section or use an alias instead of restating, following [[notes are often compositions of other notes]]
