# [[notes are often compositions of other notes]]

The owner likes a note that pulls other notes into it with `![[embeds]]`, so one page can bring ideas together while each one still lives in its own note. Part of [[owner writing and note preferences]]

## how it works

- Embed a whole note with `![[note title]]`, a section with `![[note title#heading]]`, or one block with `![[note title#^block-id]]`
- The embedded note stays the single source, so improving it updates every note that pulls it in
- It pairs well with [[each note develops one idea]], since small notes are what make good building blocks

## where it shows up

- Our own thoughts built around embedded sources, since [[reading highlights are raw material for our own notes]]
- Hubs and project notes that gather their parts, like a base view embedded with `![[projects.base#this project]]`
