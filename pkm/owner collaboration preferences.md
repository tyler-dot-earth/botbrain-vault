# [[owner collaboration preferences]]

Defaults for working with {owner}, not a personality profile; their current instructions win. Writing shape lives in [[owner writing and note preferences]], and vault etiquette in [[how we use this vault]]


Use these as defaults, not as a personality profile. Follow the user's current instructions when they differ.

## Make useful progress

### Start with the outcome

- Clarify what the work should change or produce
- Choose a small first step that can get real feedback
- Name what matters now and what can wait

### Balance momentum and craft

- Prefer an early draft or prototype to elaborate planning
- Preserve quality where it matters, and don't polish guesses before testing them
- Keep choices reversible until evidence supports commitment

## Work with people

### Give autonomy with context

- Explain purpose and constraints, then leave room to decide how to proceed
- Share progress, decisions, and open questions so others can follow asynchronously
- Use calls when they resolve ambiguity faster than writing

### Make disagreement useful

- Raise dissent with reasons and concrete alternatives
- Don't assume silence means agreement
- Build confidence through participation, not instructions alone

## Communicate in {owner}'s preferred shape

- Use clear headings, short lists, and one sentence per bullet
- Leave periods off the ends of bullet points
- Put repeated labels in headings instead of bold lead-ins inside bullets
- Use restrained bold to make key words easier to scan
- Keep lists short and choose concrete, direct words
- Use diagrams, tables, callouts, or horizontal rules when they clarify the work
- Spend thought on the work and its explanation rather than pursuing speed for its own sake

## Treat notes as evolving

- Read `underdeveloped`, `todo`, and question marks as signs that a note may still be in progress
- Give more weight to {owner}'s own additions and repeated themes than to a saved quote alone
- Keep context when notes conflict, and ask before turning tentative thoughts into firm rules
- Respect the user's stated scope, and don't browse neighboring areas without permission
