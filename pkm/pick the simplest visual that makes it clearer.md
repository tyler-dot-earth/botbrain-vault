# [[pick the simplest visual that makes it clearer]]

There's no house style for visuals. Use one when it makes a relationship, comparison, or workflow easier to see, and choose the simplest one that does. Part of [[owner writing and note preferences]]

## options

- Mermaid diagrams, ASCII diagrams, or ASCII tables
- Interactive formats when reading alone isn't enough
- Callouts for useful asides or emphasis, and horizontal rules for meaningful breaks
- Mixing prose, bullets, block quotes, backlinks, and embeds when each adds something
