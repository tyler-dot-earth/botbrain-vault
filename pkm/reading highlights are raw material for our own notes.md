# [[reading highlights are raw material for our own notes]]

Reading highlights and saved links are something to revisit, connect, and rewrite, not a finished store of knowledge. Part of [[owner writing and note preferences]]

## how to use them

- Treat highlight exports, like ones from Readwise, as read-only source material
- Embed the highlight itself in a note that states our own thought about it, like `![[book title#^highlight-id]]` for one highlight or `![[book title#highlights]]` for a section, so the source stays in one place and, since [[notes are often compositions of other notes]], our note is built around it
- Come back to them when they connect to something, since [[rough notes are where thinking starts]]
