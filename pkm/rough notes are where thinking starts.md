# [[rough notes are where thinking starts]]

The owner's thinking usually starts messy, with rough notes, questions, links, and side paths, and a through-line gets shaped later when it's useful. Part of [[owner writing and note preferences]]

## let ideas stay unfinished

- Keep questions, asides, and roughness when they show an idea is still developing
- Don't polish an exploratory thought into false certainty
- Mark a tentative idea as tentative, like with a callout, rather than smoothing it over

## build from parts

- Ideas grow out of focused parts that each keep enough context to stand alone, since [[each note develops one idea]]
- Better thinking matters more than collecting material or maintaining an elaborate system
